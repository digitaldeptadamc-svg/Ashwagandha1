Aswagandha
Botanical Name :

  • Withania somnifera (L) Dunal

    • Family Name :

  • Solanaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Hiremassina gida

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi –Asagandh

  • English –Winter cherry

    • Synonyms :

  • Aśvagandhā

  • Indian ginseng


    • Habitat :

  • Found throughout the dry parts of India,in waste places,on bunds in areas of upper gangetic plain,West Bengal,Bihar,Orissa

    • External Morphology :

  • Leaf : Cauline and ramal,alternate or in unequal pairs,ovate,ovate-oblong,or ovate lanceolate,entire,acute or obtuse,tapering to the base

  • Flower : Greenish yellow,subsessile,pedicellates,complete,bisexual,pentamerous,actinomorphic and hypogynous

  • Fruit: Small berry,globose,yellow or red when ripe


    • Useful Parts :

  • Mula(Root)


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa –Tikta Kasaya

  • Guna –laghu, Snigdha

  • Vipaka - Madhura

  • Virya - Usna


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Sedative

  • Antiseptic


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • As a Rasayana : Aswagandha taken with milk or ghee or oil oe warm water for fifteen days will act as Rasayana and increase the weight

  • Sosa : Gheta prepared from Aswagandha to be taken every day morning or the milk processed with Aswagandha taken with sugar helps to relieve Sosa


    • Dose

  • Curna(Powder) : 3 – 5 g


    • Formulation:

  • Aswagandhadya Ghrta

  • Aswagandhadi Kwatha
